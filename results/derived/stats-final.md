# Final paired statistics for the paper (13 October 2026)

Generated by `scripts/analysis/paper_stats.py` from `canonical_runs.csv`, `dup_audit.csv` and `pair_audit.csv`. Full machine-readable table with suggested wording for every row: `stats_final.csv`. Deterministic; re-run to refresh.

## 1. Method, fixed before looking at any p-value

- Pairing: by seed. Same seed means the same pre-deploy checkpoint and fault realisation. Checked with `checkpoint_config_hash` (column `pair`): `hash-verified` = equal for every seed; `no-hash` = the runs carry no hash (continuous regime), so pairing is by seed only and is NOT verified; `HASH-MISMATCH` = not the same checkpoint. `flagged` = rows of `pair_audit.csv` (written by the run-audit agent) that flag this pair (different results dir, different GPU, different fault map, different base canary).
- Measuring point: `final_test_acc` (after the last wave burst) for deployment runs; `end_of_run_test_acc` (log fallback for seed 42) for the continuous regime.
- Per comparison: n, per-seed diffs (A minus B, points), mean, SD, 95% t-CI (unreliable at n=3: t critical value 4.30), exact sign test one-sided (A>B), exact Wilcoxon signed-rank two-sided, paired t two-sided, Hedges g_av, ratio of |mean| to the repeat-noise floor.
- PRIMARY test: paired t, two-sided. Wilcoxon and sign are sensitivity checks. The t-test was fixed as primary because it is the only one of the three that can fall below 0.05 at n=3 or n=5; it assumes roughly normal seed differences, which cannot be checked with 3 to 5 points, so every p below is indicative.
- Holm-Bonferroni on the primary p inside three pre-defined families:
  - **H (headline wins, 15 comparisons)**: D vs the strongest equal-compute baseline in every scenario the paper presents as a win: main test D vs B-matched and D vs C'-matched; six stress cases D vs C'-matched; three realistic scenarios D vs C'-matched; continuous D vs C'-matched; CIFAR-100 D vs best C'-matched; VGG6 and ViT-tiny D vs C'-matched. VGG6 is included although it is not a win, so the family is not chosen by outcome.
  - **N (S4 limitation, 5 comparisons)**: calibrated S4 scenarios where D loses (D vs B-matched x4, D vs C'-matched on S4B5).
  - **S (all other, 64 comparisons)**: ablations, gate-fix, learning-rate effect, retraining vs none, D2, D vs B-matched elsewhere. Exploratory.
- Verdict rule (in order): SIGNIFICANT = Holm-adjusted primary p < 0.05 AND the unadjusted exact one-sided sign test p < 0.05 (so a significance label needs n >= 5 with every seed in the same direction; this second condition was added after a first pass showed that a df=2 t-test alone would label several n=3 claims significant, which the minimum-p logic of section 3 rules out; the t-only Holm result is still shown in every row and quoted in the wording); MIXED = seeds disagree in sign; NEGATIVE = mean < 0 and no seed above 0; NOISE-LEVEL = (not SIGNIFICANT, not NEGATIVE, not MIXED) and |mean| < 2 x rms_rerun_diff / sqrt(n) (0.41 at n=5, 0.53 at n=3, see section 2); CONSISTENT = the same but |mean| above that threshold.

**Revision 2026-10-13.** The first version labelled NOISE-LEVEL when the mean paired difference was below the worst single rerun-pair difference (1.22). That compared a mean over n seeds with a single-pair extreme, which is the wrong yardstick; the rerun noise of a mean over n paired seeds is rms_rerun_diff / sqrt(n). The rule is now NOISE-LEVEL iff |mean| < 2 x rms_rerun_diff / sqrt(n). The 1.22 figure is kept as the boolean column `exceeds_worst_single_rerun`; a single-seed result below 1.22 is not interpretable. Nothing else changed (same tests, families, Holm, precedence). The change corrects the yardstick; it was not made to move any p-value.

The N=10 pairs are: D no-pacing, random-order and weight-size-order ablations run twice (BD vs BD2, seeds 42/101/102 each = 9 pairs) plus C' on the g5 pattern, seed 42, run in two results dirs (1 pair). RMS rerun diff = **0.46**, 95% chi-square CI [0.32, 0.80] (df=10, zero-mean assumption); it is itself uncertain and is dominated by one pair (+1.22). Noise threshold for NOISE-LEVEL = 2 x rms / sqrt(n) = 0.41 at n=5, 0.53 at n=3 (using the CI upper bound: 0.72 and 0.93). Largest single |diff| = 1.22: a single-seed result below it is not interpretable; reported per comparison as `exceeds_worst_single_rerun`, not used for labels. Implied SD of one run = 0.32. The noise of a mean-of-n paired difference is about 0.46/sqrt(n) (n=5: 0.20; n=3: 0.26). So a 5-seed mean gap of 1 point is outside the noise of a 5-seed mean. Note: the run-to-run noise is already contained in the per-seed SD of every comparison, so the t-test accounts for it.

## 3. What cannot be significant by design

| n | min one-sided sign p | min two-sided sign p | min two-sided Wilcoxon exact p | paired t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 0.1250 | 0.2500 | 0.2500 | no floor, but needs normal diffs; t crit 4.30 |
| 5 | 0.0312 | 0.0625 | 0.0625 | no floor, but needs normal diffs; t crit 2.78 |

Consequences:

- n=3 comparisons can never reach p<0.05 under sign (0.125) or Wilcoxon (0.25). Only the t-test can, and its CI is wide. A Holm correction on top of the t-test will usually remove what is left. All n=3 claims must be written as effect sizes with consistent direction.
- n=5 can reach 0.031 (sign, one-sided) but not 0.05 two-sided Wilcoxon (0.0625). VGG6 (3 of 5 wins vs C'-matched) and ViT-tiny (4 of 5) cannot be significant on the sign test.
- The sign test and Wilcoxon values below are therefore reported but cannot be the basis of a significance claim for n=3.
