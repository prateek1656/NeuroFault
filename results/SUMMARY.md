## Notes

The n, max and stdev columns of the three duplicated rows below were recomputed by hand over the true seeds. The `Max acc.` column is the best of 156 to 195 looks at one test subset and is optimistic by construction; do not quote it. The moderate-regime max (about 47) is the peak of a noisy 1,000-image curve (final 41.6).

- **Duplicate seed counted as a seed** (`no_mitigation_moderate`, `remapping_only_moderate`, `soft_mitigation_only_moderate`): the unseeded dirs are byte-identical copies of seed 42. The n=4 means (41.6 / 41.45 / 44.42) differ from the true 3-seed means (41.57 / 41.90 / 44.13) by up to 0.45. The seeds are 42, 7 and 123.
- **`soft_mitigation_only_moderate` (44.13) vs `no_mitigation_moderate` (41.57), +2.57**: confounded twice (20 vs 15 epochs; the soft counter shows 25.9 to 26.1 million cell touches per run, so soft was not a no-op in these runs). Not evidence that soft mitigation works.
- **Superseded arms**: `remapping_only_moderate`, `layer_reset_only_moderate` (19.80, trails by 21.8; the heuristic dispatch touched 47 to 55 percent of one layer) and `combined_all_strategies_moderate` (37.93, 10 epochs against 15) ran with the DAC-clipping and ADC-range defects, the pre-fix dispatch and both remap bugs. They are a record of what that code did, not mitigation findings.
- **`resnet9_ibm_recipe_hwa_calibrated_gpu`** (45.98 final): the `max` of 88.90 is the float-phase peak. The 45.98 to 89.79 jump (+43.8) is unpaired (one seed against four) but about 350 times the fixed-arm SD.
- **`resnet9_ibm_recipe_hwa_full_calibrated_gpu`** (89.79, n=4): the stdev here is the sample stdev (n-1). `MANIFEST.md` quotes the population figure (0.107).
- **`local_check_no_dac` / `local_check_with_dac`** (24.80 / 37.40, seed 7, CPU, 500 test images, SE about 2.2 points): n=1, an A/B of the DAC defect.
- **`baseline_no_fault_scaled_check*` capacity ladder** (43.50, 47.90, 48.20): produced through the DAC/ADC defects; it does not show that capacity was the bottleneck.
- **`device_sttmram_*`** are numerically identical to the default device on CrossSim; no device comparison exists.
